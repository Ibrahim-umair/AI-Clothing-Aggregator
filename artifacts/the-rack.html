The Rack

Every brand lists things its own way. This is what it looks like once everything is forced through one schema — same fields, same taxonomy, regardless of which of the 15 stores it came from.

The taxonomy behind this page

Every product is filed under one shared tree, no matter which brand it came from:

  • Gender — Men / Women / Unisex
  • Category — Upperwear, Bottomwear, Footwear, Accessories, Ethnic Wear
  • Subcategory — T-Shirt, Polo, Shirt, Trouser, Belt, etc.

Chips shown greyed out are real branches of the schema with no demo listing behind them yet — most of Ethnic Wear is deliberately deferred (unstitched suits open up a much larger fabric/stitching taxonomy of their own).
